Certifications · 3 credentials

Training is useful when it changes how you work

I included more than the certificate names here. Each section explains what I studied and where that knowledge showed up again in a project, shop, lab, or workplace visit.

Safety credential

OSHA 10 — General Industry

I completed OSHA 10 before spending more time around tools, machines, and industrial equipment. The course covered PPE, emergency procedures, hazard recognition, and machine guarding. Afterward, I started noticing the safety decisions built into a shop or factory before the actual work even begins.

Engineering software

MATLAB

MATLAB introduced me to numerical computation, scripts, variables, graphs, and data analysis. What clicked for me was the ability to turn one calculation into a repeatable process, test several values, and see the result instead of only reading it.

Manufacturing coursework

ToolingU

ToolingU gave me a foundation in the language and routines used around manufacturing. That background made later field trips and hands-on projects easier to follow because I understood more of what workers and engineers were describing.
