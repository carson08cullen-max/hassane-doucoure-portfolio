More than a line on my résumé

Choose an experience to see what I did, what I learned, and the photos or videos that go with it.

I joined CARELab because I wanted to see what engineering and technology look like when the problem is not a machine, but something that affects an entire community. My group focused on affordable housing in Charlottesville. We spoke with the mayor and local housing leaders, studied what had worked in other cities, and kept coming back to one question: would this idea actually help the people it was meant for?

We used AI to organize research and test ideas, but I learned quickly that it could not replace judgment. I had to rewrite prompts, question weak answers, compare claims with real sources, and explain why our proposal fit Charlottesville. Our final concept centered on a small modular-housing pilot paired with services such as counseling, child care, financial education, and job support.

The hardest part was turning a big issue into a proposal that people could discuss seriously. Presenting our work pushed me to explain the cost, location, audience, and tradeoffs instead of stopping at a good-sounding idea.
